📖 Definitions bank

THE WORDS THAT SCORE

Exact definitions, one line each.

TermDefinition
Sine / cosine / tangentRatios in a right-angled triangle: sin = O/H, cos = A/H, tan = O/A (SOH CAH TOA).
Sine ruleRelates sides to the sines of opposite angles: a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C. Use for non-right triangles when you have a side-opposite-angle pair.
Cosine rulea² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A. Use for non-right triangles with two sides and the included angle, or all three sides.
Angle of elevationThe angle measured up from the horizontal to a point above.
Angle of depressionThe angle measured down from the horizontal to a point below.
ArcA section of the circumference of a circle, cut off by a central angle θ.
SectorThe "pizza slice" of a circle bounded by two radii and an arc.
Perpendicular bisectorA line that cuts a segment into two equal parts at a right angle. Every point on it is equidistant from both endpoints.
Voronoi diagramSplits a plane into cells so each point belongs to the nearest site. Edges are perpendicular bisectors between sites.
Nearest neighbour interpolationEstimating an unknown value at a point by taking the value of the nearest known site (its Voronoi cell).

🔢 Key facts

NUMBERS TO HAVE LOADED

  • 360° = 2π radians, so 180° = π, 90° = π/2, 60° = π/3, 45° = π/4, 30° = π/6.
  • SOH CAH TOA: sin 30° = 0.5, cos 60° = 0.5, tan 45° = 1, sin 90° = 1.
  • Sine rule ambiguous case: given two sides and a non-included angle (SSA), the angle can be A or 180° − A. Two triangles are possible — state both.
  • Area of a triangle (1/2)ab sin C is also just (1/2) × base × height — but use the sin C form when you don't know the height.
  • Voronoi edge = perpendicular bisector between two sites. A new site only redraws the cells around its immediate neighbours.
  • Toxic-waste dump problem: find the point (or site) that minimises the distance to the nearest site — the intersection of Voronoi edges, or the site with the smallest max-distance.
🎯 Worked examples

SIX QUESTIONS, STEP BY STEP

Model answers with the trap that catches people on each one.

1. Find the distance between (1, 2, 3) and (4, 6, 15).
d = √((4−1)² + (6−2)² + (15−3)²) = √(9 + 16 + 144) = √169 = 13.
Trap: it's three differences, not two. The 9−16−144 = 169 = 13² is a classic 3-4-12-13 set they reuse on purpose.
2. Find the volume of a sphere of radius 6 cm.
V = (4/3)π(6³) = (4/3)π(216) = 288π cm³.
Trap: radius is cubed, not squared. Volume answers are cubic units — a squared answer is an instant red flag.
3. In a triangle, A = 30°, a = 10 and B = 45°. Find b.
Sine rule: b = a·sin B / sin A = 10·sin 45° / sin 30° = 10(0.7071)/0.5 = 14.14.
Trap: each side pairs with the sine of its opposite angle. Side a is opposite angle A, b opposite B — don't cross them.
4. Two sides are 5 and 7, with included angle 60°. Find the third side.
Cosine rule: a² = 5² + 7² − 2(5)(7)cos 60° = 25 + 49 − 70(0.5) = 74 − 35 = 39, so a = √39 ≈ 6.24.
Trap: cosine rule needs the included angle (between the two known sides). If the angle isn't between them, it's sine rule (and possibly ambiguous).
5. A circle has radius 8 cm. Find the arc length for a central angle of 45°.
θ = 45° = π/4 rad. l = rθ = 8(π/4) = 2π ≈ 6.28 cm.
Trap: using θ = 45 directly gives l = 360 cm — absurd for a radius-8 circle. Convert to radians first.
6. For the same circle, find the area of the 45° sector.
A = (1/2)r²θ = (1/2)(64)(π/4) = 8π ≈ 25.13 cm².
Trap: sector area is (1/2)r²θ, not r²θ — that missing half is the single most common slip on this topic.
✏️ Self-test

QUICK QUIZ — 6 QUESTIONS

No notes. Pick an answer and get told right away.

Q1. The distance from the origin to (3, 4, 12) is:

✅ 13. √(9 + 16 + 144) = √169 = 13.
❌ √(3² + 4² + 12²) = √169 = 13.

Q2. sin 30° = ?

✅ 0.5. sin 30° = ½.
❌ sin 30° = ½ (a 30-60-90 triangle's short side is half the hypotenuse).

Q3. The area of a triangle with sides 6 and 8 and included angle 30° is:

✅ 12. (1/2)(6)(8)sin 30° = 24 × 0.5 = 12.
❌ Area = (1/2)ab sin C = (1/2)(6)(8)(0.5) = 12.

Q4. The arc length of a radius-10 circle for a 90° angle is:

✅ 5π. 90° = π/2, so l = 10(π/2) = 5π.
❌ Convert first: 90° = π/2, then l = rθ = 10(π/2) = 5π.

Q5. The volume of a cone with radius 3 and height 4 is:

✅ 12π. V = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3)π(9)(4) = 12π.
❌ V = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3)π(3²)(4) = 12π.

Q6. The surface area of a sphere of radius 5 is:

✅ 100π. SA = 4πr² = 4π(25) = 100π.
❌ Surface area is 4πr² (that's 100π); (500/3)π would be the volume.
